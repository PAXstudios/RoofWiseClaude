How it works

Roof to claim packet in four moves.

01 / SHOOT

Walk the slopes

Guided multi-photo capture with a camera HUD built for gloves and glare. Pitch gauge and voice notes included.

02 / DETECT

AI reads the pixels

Every photo is scanned across all 13 damage categories. Each finding gets a bounding box, severity, and a 0–100 confidence score.

03 / VERDICT

HAAG does the arguing

Material-specific functional-damage thresholds produce a per-slope and roof-level verdict with written reasoning.

04 / PACKET

Send the evidence

A claim packet with annotated photos, storm data, thresholds met, and signatures — ready for the carrier.
